Privacy Policy — BudgetEdit Publisher

Last updated: 12 July 2026

BudgetEdit Publisher is an internal publishing tool operated by TheBudgetEditStore (UK). Its sole purpose is to publish TheBudgetEditStore's own marketing content (images, carousels and reels) to TheBudgetEditStore's own Instagram professional account (@thebudgeteditstore) using Meta's Instagram API.

Who uses this app

Only TheBudgetEditStore uses this app, on its own Instagram account. It is not offered to the public, has no sign-up, and is not used to access any other person's or business's data.

What data the app accesses

  • The connected Instagram account's basic profile information (account ID and username).
  • The content we ourselves upload for publishing, and its publishing status and permalink.

The app does not access, collect or store data about any other Instagram users, followers, or visitors to this website.

How data is stored and shared

The Instagram access token is stored securely as an encrypted secret in our publishing infrastructure. We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties. Media files are hosted on this website only for the moment of publishing.

Data deletion

To request deletion of any data held by this app, or to ask any question about this policy, contact us at kranthi.kumarmca22@gmail.com. You can also remove the app's access at any time from the connected Instagram account's settings (Apps and websites).

Changes

If the app's purpose or data handling changes, this page will be updated first.
